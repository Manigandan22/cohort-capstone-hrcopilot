# Employee Documents Policy

**Category:** DOCUMENTS
**Applies to:** All employees, managers, and HR admins

## 1. Where documents are stored

Employee documents are stored in CB Nest under My Documents. Each document has a
document type: APPOINTMENT, PAYSLIP, TAX, or OTHER.

## 2. Uploading your own documents

- Employees can upload their own documents from My Documents.
- Supported file formats are .pdf, .txt, .md, .doc and .docx.
- Documents you upload yourself should use the document type OTHER unless HR asks
  you to use a different type.
- Do not upload documents that belong to another person.

## 3. Documents uploaded by HR or your manager

HR admins and managers can upload documents to an employee's record, such as
appointment letters, tax documents and payslips. You receive a notification when a
document is uploaded to your record by someone else.

## 4. Deleting documents

Employees can delete only documents of type OTHER. Appointment, payslip and tax
documents cannot be deleted by employees; contact HR through a ticket if one of these
documents is incorrect.

## 5. Payslips

Payslips are provided as password-protected PDF files. Never share your payslip
password or forward payslips outside approved company systems.
