# Leave Policy

**Category:** LEAVE
**Applies to:** All full-time employees of NovaWorks Technologies

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

Every full-time employee receives the following leave allocation per calendar year:

| Leave type | Days per year |
|---|---|
| Casual leave (CASUAL) | 12 days |
| Sick leave (SICK) | 10 days |
| Earned leave (EARNED) | 15 days |

Your remaining balance for each leave type is shown on the Leaves page in CB Nest.
Leave cannot be taken when the remaining balance for that leave type is insufficient.

## 2. Casual leave

Casual leave is meant for personal work and short absences. Planned casual leave
should be requested at least 2 working days in advance.

## 3. Sick leave

Sick leave covers illness and medical appointments. Emergency sick leave must be
informed to your reporting manager before 10:00 AM on the day of absence, and the
leave request must be submitted in CB Nest as soon as you are able to.
Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate uploaded
under My Documents with document type OTHER.

## 4. Earned leave

Earned leave is meant for planned vacations. Earned leave of 5 days or more should be
requested at least 2 weeks in advance so the team can plan coverage.

## 5. Half-day leave

Employees can apply for half-day leave for any leave type.

- A half-day leave must start and end on the same date.
- Choose either the first half (FIRST_HALF) or the second half (SECOND_HALF) of the day.
- A half-day leave deducts 0.5 day from the selected leave balance.

## 6. How leave days are counted

A full-day leave request counts every calendar day from the start date to the end
date, both inclusive. Overlapping leave requests are not allowed: you cannot submit a
new request for dates that already have a leave request.

## 7. Approval workflow

- Leave requests from employees and managers are created with status PENDING.
- A manager or HR admin approves or rejects the request. The leave balance is
  deducted only when the request is approved.
- Leave applied by an HR admin for themselves is auto-approved.
- Once approved or rejected, a request cannot be approved or rejected again.

## 8. Unused leave

Unused casual and sick leave lapses at the end of the calendar year. Up to 10 days of
unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year.
