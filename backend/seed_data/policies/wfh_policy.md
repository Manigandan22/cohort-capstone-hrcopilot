# Work From Home Policy

**Category:** WFH
**Applies to:** All employees whose role can be performed remotely

## 1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work from home up to
2 days per week. Employees on probation need explicit approval from their reporting
manager for every work-from-home day.

## 2. Approval

Work from home requires prior approval from your reporting manager. Planned
work-from-home days should be agreed with your manager at least 1 working day in
advance.

## 3. Recording WFH days

On a work-from-home day, clock in on CB Nest with work mode WFH. WFH days appear on
the Team Calendar so that your team can see who is remote.

## 4. Expectations while working from home

- Be available on official communication channels during working hours.
- Use only company-approved devices and a secure network (see the Data Security policy).
- Attend all scheduled meetings with your camera on when requested.

## 5. Exceptions

Additional work-from-home days for medical or family reasons can be approved by your
reporting manager together with HR.
