# Attendance Policy

**Category:** ATTENDANCE
**Applies to:** All employees

## 1. Working hours

The standard working day is 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Asia/Kolkata time), Monday to Friday.

## 2. Clock-in and clock-out

Every employee must clock in on CB Nest at the start of the working day and clock out
at the end of the day. When clocking in, choose your work mode:

- PRESENT: working from the office.
- WFH: working from home (see the Work From Home Policy).

## 3. Late login

A clock-in after 9:00 AM is automatically marked as LATE in CB Nest.

- Being late occasionally is recorded but does not reduce your leave balance.
- If you are marked LATE 3 or more times in a calendar month, your reporting manager
  will discuss it with you and it may be noted in your performance review.
- If you expect to be late, inform your reporting manager before 9:00 AM.

## 4. Missed clock-in

If you forget to clock in, raise an HR ticket in CB Nest on the same day explaining
the reason. A day with no clock-in and no approved leave is treated as ABSENT.
